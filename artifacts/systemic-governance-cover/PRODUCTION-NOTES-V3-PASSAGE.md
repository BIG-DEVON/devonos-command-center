# The Passage — final front-cover concept

## Concept

The cover makes the word **Through** visible rather than illustrating AI.

A cobalt signal crosses a compact institutional structure through a deliberate
band of negative space. It exits in muted gold and ends at one small point. The
structure moves from solid columns to outlines, suggesting an institution becoming
more transparent as reform passes through it.

- Ivory field: public space, clarity, and confidence
- Navy structure: the existing institution
- Open passage: transparency and reform capacity
- Cobalt signal: digital transformation
- Gold endpoint: improved services and public trust

## Design system

- Provisional trim: 6 × 9 inches
- Bleed: 0.125 inch on all sides
- Resolution: 300 pixels per inch
- Title: New York, semibold
- Supporting copy: Avenir Next
- Palette: warm ivory, institutional navy, restrained cobalt, matte ochre-gold
- Visual occupancy: approximately 20–25 percent, preserving a large uninterrupted
  field of negative space

The artwork is deterministic vector geometry rather than generated imagery. This
keeps the mark, spacing, line weights, and typography precise at print resolution.

## Production status

The author name was not supplied and is intentionally absent. A complete paperback
wrap still requires the author name, printer/platform, final trim size, page count,
paper stock/color, binding, and interior ink choice. The final press PDF should be
built on the printer's exact template; live type should then be outlined or embedded.

## Optional physical finish

The flat CMYK design is complete without specialty production. For a premium
edition, use soft-touch matte lamination, blind embossing on the institutional
structure, and spot gloss or restrained foil only on the short gold segment and dot.
