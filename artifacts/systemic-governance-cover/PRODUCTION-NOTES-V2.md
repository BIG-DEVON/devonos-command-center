# Minimal cover — “The Trust Lens”

## Design logic

The second direction responds directly to the request for a cleaner,
Pinterest-level editorial cover. A warm ivory field creates substantial negative
space. One restrained lens converts scattered lines into an ordered grid,
representing systemic alignment, transparency, accountability, and public trust.

The typography uses New York for an authoritative but contemporary title and
Avenir Next for quiet supporting copy. No author line appears because an author
name has not yet been supplied.

## Final image-generation prompt

Redesign the cover as dramatically cleaner, quieter, Pinterest-level contemporary
editorial minimalism for a serious book about governance quality, digital
transformation, AI-enabled public-sector reform, transparency, service delivery,
and public trust. Use the supplied Pinterest boards only as references for
restraint, negative space, thumbnail clarity, and contemporary polish; copy no
existing cover or layout. Use a nearly empty warm-ivory uncoated-paper field. Place
one compact “trust lens” motif in the lower portion: a clear circular lens passes
over sparse, slightly misaligned navy and muted-teal lines with a few tiny nodes;
inside the lens only, the lines resolve into a perfectly ordered transparent grid,
with one tiny warm-gold signal marking successful reform. Keep at least 65 percent
of the cover visually empty and the upper half completely uncluttered for separate
typography. Use Swiss/editorial restraint, precise geometry, delicate ink lines,
subtle refraction, and refined paper texture. Artwork only: no text, letters,
numbers, logos, watermark, border, spine, back cover, perspective, book mockup,
large architecture, charts, busy panels, dark full-page background, dramatic glow,
robots, brains, hands, flags, globes, locks, binary code, circuit boards,
dashboards, stock icons, or sci-fi imagery.

## Production status

This is a front-cover concept at a provisional 6 × 9 inch trim. A complete
printer-ready wrap still requires the author name, printer/platform, final trim,
page count, paper stock/color, binding, and interior ink choice.
